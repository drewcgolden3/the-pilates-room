The studio

Pilates that starts with your body, not a routine.

Most people arrive because something hurts, something changed, or something they love doing has started to cost them. A back that complains after a round of golf. A shoulder that never quite came back. A body six weeks after a C-section.

Questions

Before you call.

Do I need to be fit or flexible to start?

No. Most people arrive because something hurts or because they have been away from exercise for a while. Every session is private, so the work starts wherever your body actually is.

I have back pain. Is Pilates safe for me?

Back pain is the single most common reason people come to the studio. Pilates builds strength without loading the joints, which is why it suits bodies that cannot simply go and lift heavier. Michele will talk through your history before you begin.

What happens in the free consultation?

Thirty minutes, at no charge. You talk through your goals, your injuries and what you want to get back to doing, and you leave with a clear recommendation. There is nothing to buy at the end of it.

Do you teach group classes?

The studio is private and semi-private only — one person, or two. There is no class floor and no crowded schedule.

Can I train with a partner?

Yes. Semi-private sessions are $45 per person for the same hour and the same attention.

Where do I park?

There is free parking behind the building at 410 High Street, in downtown Portsmouth.

Do you work with women who are pregnant or postpartum?

Yes, including C-section recovery and diastasis recti. Pre and postnatal work is a regular part of the studio.
