About

Michele McCauley

Michele has taught Pilates since 2008, and has run The Pilates Room in downtown Portsmouth since 2013. She studied under Kathy Van Patten at the Movement Center of Boston, who she still calls her mentor, and trained for her 200-hour yoga certification at the Nosara Yoga Institute in Costa Rica.

Her background runs through muscle anatomy, movement and the body–mind connection, and through triathlon training, running, cycling, swimming, dancing and surfing. She is also a Johnson & Wales culinary graduate, which is how nutrition — and eventually the recipe blog — became part of the studio.

What that adds up to in a session is someone who can watch how you move, work out why it hurts, and know which piece of apparatus will help.
