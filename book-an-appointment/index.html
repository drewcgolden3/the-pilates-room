Book an Appointment

Booking is a phone call.

There is no online calendar, and that is deliberate. Michele wants to hear what is going on with your body before she puts you on a reformer.

Call (603) 817-4829

  1. 1
    Call the studio

    Ring (603) 817-4829 and tell Michele what you are hoping to work on. If she is teaching, leave a message and she will call you back the same day.

  2. 2
    Book your free consultation

    Thirty minutes, at no charge. You talk through your history, your injuries and what you want to get back to doing.

  3. 3
    Agree on a plan

    You leave with a clear recommendation — how often, which apparatus, and roughly how long before it feels different. Nothing to buy on the spot.

  4. 4
    Schedule your first session

    Sessions run Monday to Friday by appointment. Park free behind the building and arrive a few minutes early.

Prefer to write? Email info@thepilatesroomnh.com and include a phone number — Michele will call you back.
