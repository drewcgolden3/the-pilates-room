Glazed Chicken Thighs, Green Beans and Quinoa

Easy recipe for chicken thighs

Main Course · Mediterranean

Total
35 min
Serves
4 people

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 2 tbsps coconut aminos
  • 1 lemon juiced plus more for garnish
  • 2 cloves garlic minced
  • 1/2 tsp salt to taste
  • 1/2 tsp pepper to taste
  • 1 1/2 lbs chicken thighs skin and bone-in
  • 1/2 cup quinoa
  • 4 cups green beans

Method

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 400 degrees

  2. 02

    Whisk together the coconut aminos, maple syrup, garlic, salt and pepper. Marinate the chicken thighs in the marinade for 15 minutes.

  3. 03

    Mean awhile, make the quinoa according to the instructions.

  4. 04

    Place the marinated chicken on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Cook for 30 minutes or until the chicken is tender.

  5. 05

    Season the green beans with olive oil, salt and pepper. Add them to the oven for the last 15 minutes.

  6. 06

    Divide quinoa, green beans, and the chicken thighs. Garnish with the lemon slices.

A whole meal in 30 minutes and it's very healthy and delicious too!
