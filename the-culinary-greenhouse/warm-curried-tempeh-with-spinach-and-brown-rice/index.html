Curried Tempeh with Spinach and Brown Rice

Vegan Tempeh curry dish

Main Course · Main Dish · Indian · Plant-Based · Vegan · Vegetarian

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Serves
4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown rice
  • 2 tsps avocado oil
  • 1/2 onion chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger minced
  • 1 pkg tempeh crumbled
  • 1/2 tsp tumeric
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 Tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 can coconut milk full fat
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 1 lime juiced

Method

  1. Cook brown rice. Set aside.
  2. 01

    Heat oil in pan. Cook onion for 2-3 minutes until golden. Add ginger and cook 1 min.

  3. 02

    Add tempeh, breaking up and cooking until browned. Add coconut milk and coconut aminos and cook for 5-10 minutes.

  4. Add in spinach and lime juice and stir until spinach wilts.
  5. Serve with rice
  6. Optional: Use ground turkey in place of tofu
This curry dish is healthy and delicious! It will please any crowd and is plant-based and vegan. It can be made into a meat version using ground turkey.
