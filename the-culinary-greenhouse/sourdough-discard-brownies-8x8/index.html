Fudgy Sourdough Discard Brownies (Easy 8×8 Recipe)

Do you have extra sourdough discard sitting in your fridge? Don't throw it away! These Sourdough Discard Brownies are the ultimate way to use your leftover starter.

Servings 12 peopleCourse DessertCuisine Plant-BasedCuisine Vegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Resting Time6 hr
Total25 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sourdough discard unfed, room temperature
  • 3 large eggs room temperature
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil melted
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Prep the Oven and Pan Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease an 8x8-inch baking pan and line it with parchment paper, leaving a bit of overhang on the sides. This makes it incredibly easy to lift the brownies out of the pan once they cool.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Mix the Wet In a large mixing bowl, beat together the 3 eggs, ½ cup of sourdough discard, ¼ cup of oil, ¼ cup of melted butter, and 1 tsp of vanilla extract. Whisk until the mixture is smooth and fully combined.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Add the Dry Ingredients Whisk in the ½ cup of flour, ½ tsp of baking powder, ¼ tsp of salt, and ⅓ cup of cocoa powder. Stir gently until the dry ingredients are just incorporated into the wet batter. Be careful not to overmix, as overmixing can make the brownies cakey instead of fudgy.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Fold in the Chocolate Chips For the final step, grab your spatula and gently fold in the 1 cup of chocolate chips until they are evenly distributed throughout the thick batter.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Bake to Perfection Pour the batter into your prepared 8x8 pan and smooth the top. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes. Test the center with a toothpick; they are done when the toothpick comes out clean or with just a few moist crumbs attached.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Cool and Serve Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before lifting them out using the parchment paper edges. Slice into squares and enjoy!

  7. Baker's Tips for Success
  8. 7

    The Toothpick Test: For an extra fudgy texture, pull the brownies out when the toothpick has a few moist crumbs. If it comes out completely wet, they need a few more minutes

  9. 8

    Parchment Paper Hack: Lining the pan prevents the brownies from sticking to the bottom and allows you to make perfectly clean cuts on a cutting board later.

  10. 9

    Sourdough Hydration: This recipe is optimized for a standard 100% hydration starter (equal parts flour and water by weight).

Equipment

  • 8x8 inch pan

Notes

Chef's Notes & Troubleshooting 🥣 Sourdough Discard Tips Age of Discard: You can use fresh discard or starter that has been sitting in the fridge for up to two weeks. Older discard will add a deeper, more complex flavor profile, while fresh discard keeps the taste very mild. Temperature: Try to bring your discard to room temperature for about 15-20 minutes before mixing. Cold discard can shock the melted butter, causing it to prematurely solidify and clump in the batter. 🧈 Butter Textures Matter Why Two States?: This recipe uniquely uses both softened and melted butter. Melted butter creates that ultra-dense, fudgy texture, while the softened butter provides just enough structure so the brownies don't turn into complete puddle-like grease. Make sure your melted butter is warm, not piping hot, so it doesn't accidentally cook the eggs. 🍫 Customizing the Sweetness No Added Sugar: Because this specific recipe relies entirely on the chocolate chips for sweetness (instead of adding extra granulated sugar), the type of chocolate you choose matters. For a sweeter brownie, use milk chocolate chips . For a dark, rich, adult-style brownie, use 60% or 70% bittersweet chocolate chips . ⏱️ How to Avoid Cakey Brownies Watch the Clock: Oven calibration varies wildly. Start checking your brownies at the 22-minute mark. The Toothpick Test Rule: For cake, you want a clean toothpick. For fudgy brownies, you actually want a few moist crumbs attached to the toothpick. If the toothpick comes out completely clean, they are slightly overbaked (but will still taste delicious!). 📦 Storage & Freezing Instructions Room Temperature: Store baked brownies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days. Refrigerator: Keeping them in the fridge extends their shelf life to 6 days and makes them incredibly dense and chewy. Freezing: Wrap individual squares tightly in plastic wrap, then place them in a freezer-safe Ziploc bag. Freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw at room temperature for 1 hour or microwave for 15 seconds for a warm, gooey treat.
