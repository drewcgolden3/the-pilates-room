Salmon Cobb Salad

Healthy Salmon Cobb Salad

Salad · Mediterranean

Total
30 min
Serves
4 people

Ingredients

  • 4 pieces wild caught salmon
  • 2 tbsps olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsps fresh dill
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 eggs boiled and sliced in half
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 2 potatoes sliced thin
  • 1/2 lb asparagus
  • 2 cups fresh greens
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. 01

    Make the avocado dressing in a blender. Add avocado, oil, lemon juice, salt and water. Blend until smooth. Set aside

  2. 02

    Heat oven to 400 degrees. Brush salmon with oil, salt, pepper, dill and lemon juice. Place on baking sheet and bake for 10-15 minutes, or until salmon flakes easily with fork.

  3. 03

    Place potatoes and asparagus on a separate baking sheet, add oil, salt and pepper. Bake 25-30 minutes or until fork tender.

  4. 04

    Serve salmon, eggs (sprinkle with paprika), potatoes, asparagus and drizzle with avocado dressing.

A version of a classic Cobb salad made with salmon, asparagus, avocado dressing, potatoes, and boiled eggs. Surprise your guests with this yummy Cobb salad!
