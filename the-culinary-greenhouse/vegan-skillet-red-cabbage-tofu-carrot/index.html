Tofu Veggie Sauté with Coconut Korma Sauce

vegan dish

Main Dish · Indian · Vegan · Vegetarian

Total
25 min
Serves
4 people

Ingredients

  • 1/2 block extra firm tofu pressed to remove liquid
  • 3 cloves garlic minced
  • 2 tbsps ginger minced
  • 1 cup carrot grated
  • 1 cup broccoli slaw
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 2 cups red cabage thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup coconut korma sauce *use any jarred sauce or make recipe below ahead of time
  • 2 tbsps coconut aminos
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper
  • 1/4 tsp sesame seeds
  • 1 cup brown rice cooked
  • 1 tsp oil or butter
  • 1 yellow onion chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 tsp ginger minced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 can full fat coconut milk
  • 3 tbsps almonds ground or almond butter
  • 1/2 tsp salt more or less to taste

Method

  1. 01

    In a large skillet, heat sesame oil, add ginger and garlic and cook for 1-2 minutes. Add the tofu and cook for 2 minutes on each side or until browned.

  2. 02

    Add the carrot, broccoli, red cabbage, coconut aminos, coconut korma sauce*, red pepper, salt and pepper. Cook 5-6 minutes, medium high heat or until heated through. Serve over brown rice and garnish with sesame seeds.

  3. 03

    Cook brown rice and set aside.

  4. 04

    For the coconut korma sauce: Heat oil in pan, add garlic and ginger. Cook for 1 minute.

  5. 05

    Add the garam masala, cumin, and turmeric. Cook 1-2 minutes or until fragrant.

  6. 06

    Add tomato paste. Cook 2 minutes until tomato looks pasty.

  7. 07

    Add the coconut milk and ground almonds. Cook 10 minutes until sauce is thickened. Season with salt. Set aside

If your vegan, you will love the saltiness, crunch and depth that comes in this dish! The tofu caramelizes nicely with the sauce, garlic, ginger, ad sesame oil. Makes for a great dinner or lunch! Options include, using chicken or thinly sliced steak for meat lovers
