Kale Salad with Pistachios

Plant-based, Vegan, Vegetarian

Salad · Plant-Based · Vegan · Vegetarian

Prep
15 min
Serves
4 people

Ingredients

  • 4 cups kale
  • 2 Tbps olive oil
  • 1 lemon juiced
  • 3 cloves garlic Chopped fine
  • 3 Tbps nutritional yeast
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup pistachios raw and shelled

Method

  1. 01

    Wash and chop kale

  2. 02

    In a large mixing bowl, add kale and olive oil. Start to mix using either your hands or tongs and rub oil into kale until it turns bright green.

  3. 03

    Add the lemon juice, nutritional yeast, garlic and salt. Continue to mix until well blended.

  4. 04

    Add pistachios and serve.

  5. 05

    Refrigerate any leftovers for 3-4 days.

This is my favorite kale salad! Easy to make and very delicious:)
