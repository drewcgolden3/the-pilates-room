Oven Baked Chicken on Kale Salad with Carrot Ginger Dressing

Main Dish · Mediterranean

Prep
15 min
Serves
4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs chicken breast
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 5 cups kale
  • 2 tbsp dried cranberries
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin seeds
  • 1/2 red onion finely chopped
  • 1/2 avocado diced
  • 1/2 cup radishes finely sliced
  • 1/2 red beet grated
  • 1/2 cup carrot grated
  • 2 tbsps. olive oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1 carrot
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp ginger grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 400 degrees. Place chicken on pan and brush with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Add the chickpeas and the carrot, brush with oil, salt and pepper and bake for 25 minutes.

  2. 02

    Make the dressing in a blender with roasted carrot, oil, water, vinegar, ginger, and salt. Set aside

  3. 03

    Make the salad with Kale and the dried cranberries, pumpkin seeds, grated beet, carrot, red onion, avocado, radish. Toss well with the dressing, mixing until the kale turns bright green.

  4. 04

    Serve with sliced chicken and chickpeas on top of the kale salad.

This delicious kale salad is dressed with a carrot ginger dressing and deserves applause from the other ingredients which lead to a nice crunch, tangy and salty flavors. It reminds me of fall but it's still summer so enjoy the oven baked chicken on top of this robust kale salad and get ready to WOW your guests!
