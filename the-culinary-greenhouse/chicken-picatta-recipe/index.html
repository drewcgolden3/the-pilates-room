Chicken Piccata with broccoli

Chicken Piccata with broccoli

Servings 4 peopleCourse Main DishCuisine Italian
Total27 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken
  • 2 tbsps olive oil
  • 2 tbsps unsalted butter cold
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 2 tbps capers
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 8 oz linguine
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups brocolli cut into florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon cut into thin slices

Method

  1. 1

    Cook pasta, according to instructions. Add salt to water before it boils. Drain and set aside.

  2. 2

    Heat oil in pan, add chicken, salt and pepper. Cook chicken about 4-5 minutes, on each side. Remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Make piccata sauce. Add a bit more oil to pan, add garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add chicken broth, capers, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Cook for 2-3 minutes, or until sauce is reduced by half. Swirl in cold butter until sauce thickens.

  4. 4

    Add chicken back to pan, and spoon sauce on top.

  5. 5

    In a separate pan, heat oil. Add broccoli florets, season with salt and pepper. Cook 5-6 minutes. Serve with chicken, picatta sauce and linguine. Garnish with lemon

Notes

A classic dish featuring capers in a lemon butter sauce and spooned over chicken. Sure to be a huge hit among guests! Add linguine and broccoli. This classic dish is great with fish too!!
