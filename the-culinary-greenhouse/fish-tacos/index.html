Blackened Fish Taco Bowls

Blackened Fish Taco Bowls

Servings 4 peopleCourse Main CourseCuisine Mediterranean
Total25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs. tilapia
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 1/2 tbsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup red onion diced
  • 1/2 green pepper finely diced
  • 1 lime cut into wedges
  • 2 cups black beans
  • 2 cups cauliflower rice
  • 1 cup fresh salad greens
  • 2 whole taco shells broken into pieces
  • 1/2 avocado diced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 lemon juiced
  • 1/4 cup water
  • pinch salt

Method

  1. 1

    Heat oven to 375 degrees. Brush tilapia with olive oil, chili powder, cumin, cayenne pepper, salt and pepper. Bake 10-15 minutes or until fish flakes easily.

  2. 2

    Keep diced vegetable separates and ready to plate.

  3. 3

    Rinse the black beans until water runs clear.

  4. 4

    Cook cauliflower rice, according to the instructions.

  5. 5

    Make avocado dressing by blending avocado, oil, water, lemon and salt in a high-speed blender.

  6. 6

    Serve diced red pepper, green pepper, black beans, cauliflower rice, and tilapia on a plate. Drizzle with avocado dressing, lime wedges, salad greens, and broken taco shells

Notes

Short on time but looking for flavor, this blackened fish taco bowl is right up your alley! Paired with fresh veggies, and a lemon avocado dressing, your taste buds with say yessss, more please yummy.
