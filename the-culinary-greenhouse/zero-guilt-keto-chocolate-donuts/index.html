Zero Guilt Keto Chocolate Donuts

A rich, fudgy, low-carb, and nut-free donut recipe packed with whey protein and deep cocoa flavor.

Breakfast · Dessert · American · high protein treats · keto · keto desserts · low carb breakfast · Plant-Based

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Total
25 min
Serves
6 people

Ingredients

  • For the donuts
  • 1/3 cup coconut flour
  • 2 tbsp whey protein
  • 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 cup granular sweetner monk fruit or erythritol
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3 large eggs room temperature
  • 1/4 cup butter melted
  • 6 tbsp cold brewed strong coffee
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • For the Sugar-Free Glaze
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp butter melted
  • 1-2 tbsp heavy cream room temperature
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Baking the Donuts
  2. 01

    Prep Your Oven: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Thoroughly grease a standard 6-cavity donut pan with non-stick spray or butter. [1, 2, 3]

  3. 02

    Combine the Dry Ingredients: In a medium-sized bowl, vigorously whisk together your coconut flour, whey protein powder, cocoa powder, granular sweetener, baking powder, and salt until completely free of lumps. [1, 2, 3]

  4. 03

    Whisk the Wet Ingredients: In a separate container, beat the eggs. Slowly pour in the melted butter, cooled brewed coffee, and vanilla extract, whisking continuously to avoid cooking the eggs. [1, 2, 3]

  5. 04

    Mix the Batter: Pour your liquid mixture directly into the dry ingredients. Stir with a spatula until a smooth, uniform batter forms. Note: Coconut flour absorbs liquid rapidly, so let the batter sit for 2 minutes to thicken up. [1, 2, 3]

  6. 05

    Fill the Pan: Spoon the batter evenly into your prepared donut cavities, or pipe it in using a plastic storage bag with the corner snipped off. Smooth down the tops with a damp finger. [1, 2, 3]

  7. 06

    Bake to Perfection: Bake for 15 to 18 minutes. They are ready when the tops feel firm to a gentle touch and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. [1, 2, 3]

  8. 07

    Cool Safely: Allow the donuts to cool inside the pan for 10 minutes. Carefully invert the pan over a wire rack to release them, letting them cool completely before adding any optional glaze. [1, 2]

  9. (Recipe inspired by keto baking traditions)
  10. Glazing the Donuts
  11. 08

    Sift the Dry: Sift the powdered sweetener and cocoa powder into a small bowl to remove lumps.

  12. 09

    Combine Liquids: Stir in the melted butter, vanilla extract, and 1 tablespoon of the warm heavy cream.

  13. 10

    Adjust Thickness: Whisk until completely smooth. If the glaze is too thick, add the remaining heavy cream a few drops at a time until it reaches a thick, pourable consistency.

  14. 11

    Dip the Donuts: Dip the tops of your fully cooled donuts directly into the glaze, then place them back on a wire rack to set for 10 minutes.

Let the Batter Rest: Coconut flour acts like a sponge and absorbs liquid rapidly. After mixing the wet and dry ingredients, let the batter sit on your counter for 2 full minutes. It will thicken up significantly, making it much easier to pipe or spoon into your donut pan. The Coffee Secret: Do not skip the brewed coffee! The coffee does not make the donuts taste like mocha; instead, the acidity and flavor compounds chemically intensify the cocoa powder, making the donuts taste incredibly rich and fudgy. Ensure the coffee is cooled to room temperature before mixing, or you risk scrambling the eggs. Protein Powder Choice: For the best texture, use a whey protein isolate or a whey-casein blend. Avoid using pure plant-based protein powders (like pea or hemp) in this specific recipe, as they absorb too much moisture and will make your donuts dense and dry. Nut-Free Alternative: This recipe is naturally nut-free because it uses coconut flour instead of almond flour. If you are baking for someone with a severe coconut allergy, please note that coconut flour cannot be swapped 1:1 with any other flour. Storage & Freezing: Store leftover glazed donuts in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. You can also freeze unglazed donuts for up to 3 months; simply thaw them at room temperature and apply fresh glaze before serving. (Recipe inspired by keto baking traditions.
