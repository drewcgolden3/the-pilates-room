Shrimp, White Bean, Kale Stew

Flavorful shrimp, white bean, and kale stew

Main Dish · Mediterranean

Total
30 min
Serves
4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 lb large shrimp peeled and deveined
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 leek thinly sliced
  • 1 medium zucchini or summer squash diced medium
  • 5 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 bottle clam juice
  • 1 can white beans rinsed
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 2 tbsp chives chopped
  • 3 cups kale just leaves, not stems

Method

  1. 01

    In large Dutch oven, heat the oil and sauté the leek with salt and pepper for 3 minutes, or until softened. Add the zucchini and garlic. Cook 3 minutes longer, or until squash is softened. Season with more salt and pepper.

  2. 02

    Add the clam juice, beans, and shrimp. Cover and cook for 4-5 minutes, or until shrimp is cooked through.

  3. 03

    Add the kale, lemon juice, butter, crushed red pepper and 1 Tbsp of the chives. Cook 3-4 minutes longer, until kale wilts.

  4. 04

    Serve in bowls and garnish with remaining chives and a piece of crusty bread:)

A delicious, warm hearty stew with taste of the ocean and freshness of the garden. Both shrimp and clam juice give this stew a salty ocean flavor. Other flavors include lemon, garlic, a touch of heat, and butter. The kale adds body and freshness to this delicious stew. Serve with a loaf of warm crusty bread and please all of your guests!
