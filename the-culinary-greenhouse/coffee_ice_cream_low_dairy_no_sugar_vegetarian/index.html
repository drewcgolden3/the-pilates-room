Coffee Ice Cream

Low dairy, no sugar, vegetarian

Servings 6 peopleCourse DessertCuisine Vegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Resting Time6 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 can coconut milk full fat
  • 1/2 cup almond milk
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2/3 cup monk fruit or allulose
  • 1/4 tsp xanthan gum
  • 1-1 1/2 tsp espresso powder
  • 3/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch salt

Method

  1. 1

    In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine 1 cup coconut milk and 1/2 cup almond milk. Whisk in the sweeteners. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer 30 minutes, whisking occasionally.

  2. 2

    Remove from the heat. Sprinkle the surface with xanthan gum while simultaneously whisking in to combine well.

  3. 3

    Whisk in the instant espresso, vanilla extract, and salt., until the espresso has dissolved. Let it cool to room temperature.

  4. 4

    Whip the heavy cream and remaining coconut milk until it just holds stiff peaks. Gently fold in the cooled espresso mixture until well blended.

  5. 5

    Transfer to a container and freeze until firm, about 5 to 6 hours. Or keep refrigerated for a soft mousse.
